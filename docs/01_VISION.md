# 01 — Vision

Nami is Kaif's personal AI operating system inspired by JARVIS, but grounded in real technology.

## Vision statement

Build a practical AI assistant that can talk, see the screen, remember, research, automate, create, code, and help Kaif complete digital work safely.

## Experience vision

Kaif should be able to say:

> "Nami, research this topic and give me the best plan."

> "Nami, customize my resume for this job."

> "Nami, draft a follow-up email."

> "Nami, watch my screen and tell me what is wrong."

> "Nami, prepare a Codex task to build this project."

> "Nami, trigger my n8n automation."

## Long-term vision

Nami becomes a daily command center for:

- Personal productivity
- Job search
- Client projects
- Software building
- Document creation
- Email/calendar management
- Research
- Automation
- Screen guidance
